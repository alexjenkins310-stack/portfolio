<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>The Objective</h2>
  <p>Financial statements contain a significant amount of information by nature, but evaluating performance requires understanding how individual accounts and financial ratios relate to one another over time.</p>
  <p>The objective of this project was to use Tableau to organize financial information into dashboards that make those relationships easier to identify. The broader goal was to demonstrate how financial data can be transformed into a visual story that helps management understand what is changing within a company or helps investors evaluate its financial performance and position.</p>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>Balance Sheet &amp; Financial Position</h2>
  <p>The balance sheet dashboard organizes assets, liabilities, and owners’ equity while incorporating financial measures related to liquidity, working capital, operating efficiency, and risk.</p>
  <p>The dashboard includes measures such as the current ratio, quick ratio, asset turnover, return on capital employed, gearing, interest coverage, and other working-capital indicators. Presenting these measures alongside the balance sheet provides additional context for evaluating changes in the company’s financial position across the period analyzed.</p>
  <p>Gearing was a term that was new to me when completing this project. Researching the concept helped me better understand how leverage measures can be constructed and interpreted differently depending on the context. In this dashboard, the gearing measure uses cumulative liabilities relative to cumulative assets across the periods displayed, allowing the dashboard to show how the relationship between the two measures developed over time.</p>
  <p>This portion of the project allowed me to connect financial statement accounts with the ratios commonly used to evaluate liquidity, efficiency, leverage, and financial risk.</p>
</section>
